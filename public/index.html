Frequently asked questions

How do you age a deer by its jawbone?

By reading tooth replacement and wear on the lower jaw. The third premolar and the molar crests change in a predictable way as a deer gets older. Deer Ager does the reading for you and explains what it saw.

Can I age a deer from a photo?

Yes. A jawbone photo gives the most reliable answer. Photos of a whole deer give a rough age class.

What should I photograph?

Both cheek sides of the lower jaw, a top-down view of the chewing surfaces and a close-up of the premolars and first molar, in daylight with a ruler for scale. The app walks you through it.

Is my hunting data private?

Yes. Your photos, buck log and notes are visible only to you and Deer Ager support.
