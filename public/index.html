Deer Ager never just hands you a number. Every report shows the AI's confidence and the datapoints behind it, such as the wear on each molar, so you can see exactly why you got your answer instead of trusting a black box.

Honest about accuracy

Aging by tooth wear is dependable for fawns, yearlings and young adults, and less exact for mature deer. Deer Ager tells you the confidence behind every result and flags photos that are too blurry, dark or incomplete, then tells you exactly which shot to retake. A lab cementum test remains the gold standard.
